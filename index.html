Our Story

Crafting Culinary Excellence With Passion

At The Silver Spoon, we believe every meal should be a celebratory feast. Located in the cultural heart of Saptagram, Mogra (Hooghly), our restaurant brings together two of the world's most revered culinary traditions.

Our master chefs hand-pick authentic Indian spices from regional gardens and employ age-old clay tandoor techniques alongside high-flame Cantonese wok wok firing to craft dishes of unmatched depth and aroma.
